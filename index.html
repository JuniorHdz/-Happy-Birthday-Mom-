Happy Birthday, Mom! 💖

Today is a very special day because it is the day we celebrate one of the most important and beautiful people in my life — you. I honestly don't think words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me, but today I want to try.

Thank you for every hug, every smile, every piece of advice, every sacrifice, and every little thing you have done for me. There have been so many moments when you have been there for me without asking for anything in return, and I want you to know that I appreciate every single one of them.

You have taught me so much about life, about being strong, about caring for others, and about never giving up. Whenever life gets difficult, knowing that I have you in my corner makes everything feel a little easier.

You are more than just my mom. You are someone I can look up to, someone I can trust, someone who makes me feel loved, and someone who has helped shape the person I am today.

I may not always say it, and I may not always show it perfectly, but I love you more than you know. ❤️

Thank you for being my mom, my biggest supporter, and one of the greatest blessings in my life.

Happy Birthday, Mom! 🎂💐
